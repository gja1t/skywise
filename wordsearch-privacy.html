Privacy Policy for Word Search

Last updated: November 18, 2026




This policy covers Word Search (Woordzoeker, Wortsuche, Mots Mêlés, Sopa de Letras) by Skywise: the Android app and the web version at skywise.nl/wordsearch. Word Search has no ads, no purchases and no account.



On your device


Your puzzle, settings, player name and high scores are stored only on your device. The web version keeps them in your browser's local storage. None of this is sent to Skywise.



Playing together


Only when you choose "Play together", or open an invitation link, the app connects to Google Firebase, a service Skywise uses to let two devices share a puzzle. For that game it sends: a random user number that the app creates without asking anything, the name you entered (or "Player"), the puzzle and the words each player found. As with any internet connection, Google also sees your IP address. Nothing is shared with anyone except the other player, who sees your name and the words you found. There is no chat.


A game is deleted when the player who started it stops, and otherwise after 48 hours, as soon as one of the players opens the app again. The random user number stays on your device so you remain the same player; it is not linked to your name, e-mail or Google account. To have your data removed, mail info@skywise.nl. How Google handles this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.



Web version


The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, may log your IP address when you load the page: GitHub's privacy statement.
