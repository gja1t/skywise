Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Google Play Store

Last updated: November 18, 2026




This policy covers the Skywise apps Fitness Timer, Kids Timer, Kids Timer+ and Word Search (Woordzoeker). None of these apps has an account. Apart from playing Word Search together (described below), Skywise does not collect, store or receive any personal information through them. Your settings are stored only on your device.



Word Search


Word Search has no ads and no purchases. Your puzzle, settings, player name and high scores are stored only on your device.


Playing together. Only when you choose "Play together", or open an invitation link, the app connects to Google Firebase, a service Skywise uses to let two devices share a puzzle. For that game it sends: a random user number that the app creates without asking anything, the name you entered (or "Player"), the puzzle and the words each player found. As with any internet connection, Google also sees your IP address. Nothing is shared with anyone except the other player, who sees your name and the words you found. There is no chat and no account.


A game is deleted when the player who started it stops, and otherwise after 48 hours, as soon as one of the players opens the app again. The random user number stays on your device so you remain the same player; it is not linked to your name, e-mail or Google account. To have your data removed, mail info@skywise.nl. How Google handles this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.


The web version at skywise.nl/wordsearch works the same way: it keeps your puzzle, settings and high scores in your browser's local storage, and only playing together sends the data described above. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, may log your IP address when you load the page: GitHub's privacy statement.



Kids Timer+


Kids Timer+ is a paid app without ads. It does not collect or share any data.



Fitness Timer and Kids Timer: advertising


Fitness Timer and Kids Timer show ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), how you interact with the app and its ads, and diagnostic data.


In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the apps ask for your consent before any ad is requested. In Fitness Timer you can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Settings, "Privacy choices for ads". On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings.


Kids Timer only requests ads that are rated suitable for general audiences.


How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites



Fitness Timer and Kids Timer: purchases


In both apps a one-time purchase removes the ads; in Kids Timer it also unlocks the Premium features. The purchase is handled by Google Play; Skywise does not receive your payment details. Once the purchase is active, the app no longer requests or shows ads.
